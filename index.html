PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026

Trinitarian is operated by Globiq Media Technologies Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Full name and display name
  • Email address
  • Username and password (encrypted)
  • Denomination and church information (for pastors)
  • Country and city

Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage data
  • Sermon viewing and listening history
  • IP address and approximate location

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To verify pastor applications
  • To provide sermon recommendations
  • To send notifications about new sermons and live streams
  • To improve the app and develop new features
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We rely on a small number of trusted service providers, each of which processes only the data necessary for their specific function:

  • Agora - powers live audio/video streaming
  • Expo - delivers push notifications
  • Resend - sends transactional emails
  • Railway - hosts our backend and database
  • Cloudflare - provides DNS, content delivery, and routing for our website
  • Backblaze B2 - stores uploaded sermon media (audio, video, and images)
  • Paystack - processes Trinitarian Pro subscription payments for pastors. Trinitarian never receives or stores your full card details.
  • Google AdSense - displays advertisements to listeners, who use Trinitarian free of charge
  • Google AdMob - may display advertisements within our mobile app, now or in the future, to listeners who use Trinitarian free of charge

We may also share information when required by law.

4. Advertising and Cookies

Trinitarian is free for listeners to use and is supported in part by advertising. On our website, this is served through Google AdSense. Our mobile app may also display advertising, now or in the future, through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior activity, and to measure ad performance.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalisation settings, at google.com/settings/ads (for the website) or in your Android device settings under Google, Ads (for the mobile app). Trinitarian Pro subscribers (pastors) do not see advertisements.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored securely using HTTPS/TLS encryption. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Contact: privacy@trinitarian.app

7. Children's Privacy

Trinitarian is not directed at children under 13.

8. Content Ownership

All sermon content remains the intellectual property of the uploading pastor. Trinitarian holds only a non-exclusive streaming licence. All content is protected under DMCA.

9. Contact Us

  • Email: privacy@trinitarian.app
  • Website: trinitarian.app
  • Company: Globiq Media Technologies Ltd

Last updated: September 2026

1. About Trinitarian

Trinitarian is operated by Globiq Media Technologies Ltd. We provide a platform for verified, ordained pastors to share sermons with believers worldwide.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 13 years old to use Trinitarian.

3. User Accounts

  • You must provide accurate information when registering
  • You are responsible for your account security
  • We may suspend accounts that violate these terms

4. Pastor Verification

By applying as a pastor you confirm you are an ordained minister with at least 2 years of active ministry. False information will result in permanent suspension.

5. Trinitarian Pro Subscriptions

Trinitarian Pro is a paid subscription available to verified pastors only. Listeners cannot purchase Trinitarian Pro and always use Trinitarian free of charge, supported by advertising.

  • Price: $10 USD per month, billed automatically on a recurring monthly basis until cancelled.
  • What's included: live streaming, an ad-free experience, high quality audio for live broadcasts, and priority placement of your notifications to your followers.
  • Payment processing: all payments are processed securely by Paystack. Trinitarian does not receive or store your full card details.
  • Cancellation: you may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation stops future billing; you retain Pro access until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.
  • Refunds: payments already made are non-refundable, including for partial billing periods. If you believe you were charged in error, contact support@trinitarian.app.
  • Price changes: we may change the price of Trinitarian Pro from time to time. Any change will be communicated to subscribers in advance and will apply to your next billing cycle onward, not retroactively.
  • Failed payments: if a payment fails, we will attempt to notify you by email. Pro access may be suspended until payment is successfully processed.

6. Content Standards

All content must be consistent with the Christian faith. Prohibited: content promoting hatred, false teaching, cults, or harm to children.

7. Content Ownership

Pastors retain full copyright of all uploaded content. By uploading, you grant Trinitarian a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to stream and display your content.

8. DMCA

To report copyright infringement: dmca@trinitarian.app
